THE MAGICIAN
A Mood-inspired micro-looper + granular + FX for the Empress ZOIA
====================================================================

Inspired by the Chase Bliss Mood, but heavily reworked and expanded -
it takes the "capture and mangle a slice of sound" spirit and builds a
different, more open instrument around it (shared FX bus, granular on
the loop, coupled record dry/wet, true-stereo loopers).

Part of a Tarot-arcana series. The Magician "captures and transmutes":
you loop what you play, then reshape it with granular processing and a
delay/reverb effects bus. Stereo in / stereo out.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
SIGNAL ARCHITECTURE (aux-send, stereo)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

The patch is built like a small studio mixer with AUX SENDS, not a
simple series chain:

  IN (stereo) --+--> LIVE dry (direct) -----------------------> OUT
                +--> Live aux send --\
                                      >--> [DELAY -> REVERB] --> OUT   (shared WET bus)
  LOOPER --> GRANULAR --+--> Loop dry (direct) --------------> OUT
                        +--> Loop aux send --/

Key points:

* SHARED WET BUS. The Delay and the Reverb form ONE wet bus (Delay ->
  Reverb, in series). BOTH the live signal AND the loop feed it through
  independent aux sends:
      - "Live FX Snd"  = how much of the LIVE signal is sent to the FX
      - "Loop FX Snd"  = how much of the LOOP is sent to the FX
  Each source is a dry/wet crossfade (dry-direct vs aux-send), so the
  two sources can have different wet amounts while sharing a single
  Delay + Reverb (saves CPU vs duplicating the effects).

* THE GRANULAR IS NOT IN THE FX BUS. The granular is placed on the
  LOOP only, POST-looper (it grinds whatever is in the loop, live). It
  is NOT part of the Delay/Reverb bus and it does NOT touch the live
  signal. So the granular reshapes the LOOP, and the FX bus adds
  space/echo to live + loop.

* STEREO throughout. Two loopers (L and R) run in parallel for a true
  stereo loop; the sends, crossfades, delay and reverb are all stereo.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
RECORD DRY / WET  (the important bit: coupled to LOOP FX on/off)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

What gets baked into the loop when you record depends on the LOOP FX
toggle. This is intentional and avoids stacking effects:

* LOOP FX OFF  ->  RECORD WET
  The loop captures the signal WITH the effects (delay/reverb baked in).
  Rationale: with Loop FX off, the loop is NOT re-sent to the FX at
  playback, so you want the effect character frozen into the recording.
  Playback = the wet you performed, fixed.

* LOOP FX ON   ->  RECORD DRY
  The loop captures the DRY signal only. Rationale: with Loop FX on,
  the loop IS routed to the FX bus at playback (live effects applied to
  the loop). Recording dry avoids stacking (baked FX + live FX = mush /
  runaway). You get live, tweakable effects on a clean loop.

The "Loop FX Snd" knob does double duty:
  - Loop FX OFF -> sets how much WET is baked into the recording
  - Loop FX ON  -> sets the loop's live send amount to the FX bus


--------------------------------------------------------------------
HOME PAGE LAYOUT (page 0)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Organised in columns, one effect per column; knobs on top, on/off and
performance buttons on the bottom row.

  DELAY column     : Delay Feedback, Delay Time, Delay Depth, Delay Mix
  REVERB column    : Rev. Decay, Rev. Low EQ, Rev. High, Rev. Mix
  LOOPER column    : Loop Start, Loop Length, Loop Clock, Loop Level
  GRANULAR column  : Grain Pos, Grain Size, Grain Pitch, Grain Mix,
                     Grain Density
  SENDS / MASTER   : Live FX Snd, Loop FX Snd, Out Gain

  Bottom row (toggles / performance):
    Delay On | Reverb On | Loop FX | Grain On | FX On
    Loop Reverse L | Loop Reverse R | Grain Freeze

  On/off indicators use one shared colour scheme (dim = off, bright =
  on). "FX On" is the master wet on/off, "Loop FX" flips the loop
  routing + record dry/wet behaviour described above.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
STOMPSWITCHES  (tap / hold)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

  LEFT  (Rec/Dub) : TAP  = Record / Overdub
                    HOLD = Play / Stop
  MID   (Frz/Clr) : TAP  = Granular Freeze
                    HOLD = Clear / reset the loop
  RIGHT (Wet)     : TAP  = Wet (FX bus) on/off
                    HOLD = Loop FX on/off


--------------------------------------------------------------------
LOOPER REVERSE  (individual, stereo)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

The two loopers (L and R) have INDEPENDENT reverse controls
("Loop Reverse L" and "Loop Reverse R" on the home page). Reversing
only one channel splits the stereo image (one side forward, one side
backward) for wide, disorienting textures; reverse both together for a
normal full reverse. This is a feature, not a bug - it exploits the
true-stereo two-looper design.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------

* CPU: this patch runs CLOSE TO THE ZOIA'S LIMIT. The granular, the
  reverb and the delay are the heavy modules. If you hit CPU errors,
  lower the granular's grain count, or swap the Hall reverb for a
  lighter one. Adding modules on top may push it over - trim elsewhere
  first.

* MIDI: most parameters are mapped to MIDI CC (channel 12) for external
  control.

* Everything is stereo - use stereo I/O for the full effect (mono in
  will leave one side of the stereo field empty on the direct paths).
